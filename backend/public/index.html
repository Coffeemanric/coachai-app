Can I use CoachAI on multiple devices? ›
Yes — each license key works on up to 3 devices. Perfect for your phone, tablet, or sharing with a training partner.
Do I need an internet connection? ›
An internet connection is required for video analysis and AI chat. Your player profiles, drill history, and analysis results are stored locally and always available offline.

Absolutely. Payments are processed by Square, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment provider. We never see or store your card details — Square handles all payment security.
